Version 1.1 brings more ways to keep your Mac setup and controls close at hand:

• Back up saved Macs, keyboard layouts, shortcuts and saved text to Files without iCloud. Backup filenames include the local date, time and iPhone or iPad label, and remain editable.
• Preview a backup before restoring. Your previous data is kept as a recovery copy, and restored Macs ask you to sign in and confirm their identity again.
• Optionally sync keyboard layouts, shortcuts, saved text and button positions with iCloud. Review recovered alternatives when changes conflict.
• See enlarged key previews as you press keyboard-row keys, with clearer Escape and row controls.
• Find clearer guidance in Sync & Security and Mac setup.

Passwords, SSH private keys and trusted Mac identities stay local. Restore turns iCloud Sync off on this device and keeps existing iCloud data.
