# Mac Companion Community Code of Conduct

Policy status: prepared for maintainer and legal review before contribution
intake opens.

## Our standard

Mac Companion participants must make project spaces respectful, inclusive,
constructive, and safe. Treat people with empathy, assume good faith while
remaining evidence-driven, and focus criticism on ideas, behavior, and code.

Acceptable behavior includes:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experience;
- giving and receiving specific, constructive feedback;
- protecting privacy and handling security information responsibly; and
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and repairing harm after mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking,
discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, doxxing,
publishing private information, deliberate security-policy violations, or
retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents
Mac Companion or Jenny Media in a public space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or edit content, reject contributions, limit
participation, or ban a participant when needed to protect the community. They
will seek a proportionate response, protect reporter privacy where practical,
avoid conflicts of interest, and document material enforcement decisions
privately.

The private conduct-reporting route is not yet published. Until an approved
route and responsible maintainer are named, external contribution intake and
project-hosted community activity remain paused. Do not use the security route
for ordinary conduct reports unless a concern also creates a security risk.

This policy does not replace the separate security reporting instructions in
`SECURITY.md`.
