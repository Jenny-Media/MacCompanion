Three steps to your Mac.

  1. 1. Get TestFlight

    Open the invitation on your iPhone. Apple will guide you through installing TestFlight if you need it.

  2. 2. Install the beta

    Accept the invitation and install Mac Companion. New beta builds appear in TestFlight as they become available.

  3. 3. Connect and explore

    Enable Remote Login on your Mac, plus Screen Sharing for desktop access. No Mac Companion helper is required.

Beta builds can have rough edges. Share feedback through TestFlight or contact support. TestFlight purchase tests use Apple’s sandbox and do not charge real money.
