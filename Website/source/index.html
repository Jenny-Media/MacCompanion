The Mac half is already there.

Enable. Add. Connect.

No companion app or helper to install on your Mac.

  1. 01

    Enable sharing services

    In Mac System Settings, open General → Sharing. Enable Remote Login and Screen Sharing for the account you’ll use.

  2. 02

    Add your Mac

    Save its local address or private VPN address in the iPhone app. Add more than one address if you use both.

  3. 03

    Make yourself at home

    Sign in with your Mac account. Optionally save the login on your iPhone, then open your desktop whenever you need it.

Use a trusted network. Desktop and input travel through an SSH connection after you verify the Mac’s server key. For access away from home, use a private VPN such as Tailscale on both devices. Never expose Screen Sharing directly to the internet. Connection guide

Before you connect.

A few clear answers.

Do I need to install anything on my Mac?

No. Mac Companion connects to built-in Screen Sharing for Desktop and Trackpad & Keyboard. Enable Remote Login for all modes, and Screen Sharing for Desktop and Trackpad & Keyboard.

Can I connect away from home?

Yes, with an active private VPN route, such as Tailscale, on your Mac and iPhone. The app does not provide its own VPN or relay. See the network setup guide.

Does the free version expire?

No. Basic features remain free after a Pro trial ends. The optional 14-day trial does not renew or charge automatically. Lifetime Pro is a separate one-time purchase.

What syncs with iCloud?

If you opt in, saved Mac names, addresses, ports, and display/input settings sync through iCloud Keychain. Passwords, SSH private keys, server trust, and saved text stay local. Read the iCloud privacy details.

How can I get the app?

Mac Companion is currently being tested in private TestFlight. Public downloads are not available yet. Availability and compatibility.
